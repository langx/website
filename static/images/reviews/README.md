# Reviewer photos

The profile pictures beside the Google Play reviews quoted on /compare, as
the public listing shows them (play.google.com, tech.newchapter.languageXchange,
fetched 23 September 2026 and resized to 96×96 WebP). Each belongs to the
person whose review it sits beside; a reviewer with no photo of their own on
Google Play gets initials instead (see `src/lib/data/reviews.ts`). Take one
down the moment its owner asks.

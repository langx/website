Almost every language exchange app is free to download, but "free" means three very different things. **Speaky** and **InterPals** have no subscription at all and are paid for by ads. **HelloTalk** and **Tandem** give you the core app free, with ads and daily caps on extras like translation. **LangX** has no ads on its free plan and never limits replies or corrections, but caps new conversations at 5 a day.

Below is what each free tier actually includes and where it stops. We make LangX, so our own limits are listed as plainly as everyone else's. Everything below was checked against each app's help pages and app store listings in September 2026. We do not quote prices, because they change by country and by month.

If you want the broader ranking (community size, voice, safety, who each app suits), read our main list of the [best language exchange apps](/best-language-exchange-apps). This page only answers one question: what do you get without paying?

## Three kinds of "free"

1. **No subscription at all.** Everything is free and paid for by ads, with nothing to buy to remove them.
2. **Freemium with ads.** Most of the app is free, some tools have daily caps, and a subscription removes the caps and the ads.
3. **Freemium without ads.** A free plan with limits and a paid plan above it, with no advertising on either.

A fourth group is **free to try**: you start with an allowance of minutes or exercises, then pay, earn credit, or slow down.

## Apps with no subscription at all

### Speaky

[Speaky](https://www.speaky.com) is the clearest answer to "is there a completely free language exchange app?". Its US App Store listing shows no in-app purchases, and its Google Play listing shows no in-app purchases either, only ads. The seller on the App Store is now Altissia, a language-learning company, and the app was updated in September 2026.

- **Free:** partner search across 170+ languages, messaging, a community map, and AI practice partners for when nobody is online.
- **Limits:** nothing is locked, but nothing removes the Android ads either. The store listing does not describe a translation or correction tool in the chat, so bring your own dictionary.

More detail in [LangX vs Speaky](/open-source-alternative-to-speaky).

### InterPals

[InterPals](https://www.interpals.net) is a pen-pal site that has been running since 1998. Its FAQ says all its services are completely free and that the site is funded by advertising.

- **Free:** your profile, search by country, age, language and interests, and messaging.
- **Limits:** to fight spam, there is a daily cap on contacting **new** people, which rises the longer you are a member. Messages to people you already talk to are not limited. There is no correction tool, so say "here for language exchange" in your profile.

More detail in [LangX vs InterPals](/open-source-alternative-to-interpals).

## Free, with an optional membership

### Conversation Exchange

[Conversation Exchange](https://www.conversationexchange.com) is a website, not an app, and it is free to use with banner ads. It is the best free option for finding a partner **in your own city**, and also handles pen pals and online chat. An optional paid membership removes the ads and the messaging limits. There is no built-in translation or correction. See [LangX vs ConversationExchange](/open-source-alternative-to-conversationexchange).

### Slowly

[Slowly](https://slowly.app) is a pen-pal app that many people use for languages. Letters take from about half an hour to more than two days to arrive, depending on how far apart you live, which makes it one of the lowest-pressure ways to practice writing.

- **Free:** writing letters to your pen pals, without a cap on letters.
- **Limits:** a quota on how many friends you can have at once. **Slowly Plus**, the subscription, doubles quotas such as the number of friends and photo sharing. Decorative stamps can be bought with Slowly Coins, which you buy or earn by watching short ads.

See [LangX vs Slowly](/open-source-alternative-to-slowly).

## Freemium: most of the app free, extras capped

### HelloTalk

HelloTalk's free tier is generous. At the time of writing, free members get:

- **Unlimited** one-to-one text and voice messages, plus voice and video calls, which HelloTalk's FAQ says have no time limit.
- **Moments** (a public feed where native speakers correct your posts), **Voicerooms** and livestreams.
- Translation, transliteration, voice-to-text and AI correction in chat, **each with a daily cap**.

**VIP** removes the ads, lifts those caps, lets you contact up to 25 new partners a day, learn more than one language, search by gender and nearby, and turns on captions in Voicerooms and livestreams. If you lean on translation for every message, you will feel the cap. More in [LangX vs HelloTalk](/open-source-alternative-to-hellotalk).

### Tandem

Tandem's own app overview lists the free plan clearly: text chat, corrections, voice messages, and audio and video calls, plus **10 new conversations a day**, **3 translations a day**, up to **60 minutes a day** in Language Parties (its live audio rooms), one language to learn, and ads.

**Tandem Pro** raises that to 30 conversations a day, makes translation unlimited, adds an AI toolkit (grammar check, correction explanations and more), removes ads and lets you learn up to 10 languages. The translation cap is the limit beginners hit first. More in [LangX vs Tandem](/open-source-alternative-to-tandem).

### LangX

Our app, so judge this entry accordingly. LangX's free plan is a real plan rather than a trial, and it has **no ads**. Every limit below runs over a rolling 24 hours:

- **Unlimited** text messages, **unlimited replies** and **unlimited corrections**.
- **5 new conversations** you start per day.
- **20 translations** a day inside the chat.
- 15 messages read aloud a day, and 50 messages a day with a photo, video or voice note.
- One language you are learning and one you speak; filters by country, age and level.

The honest limits: 5 new chats a day is fewer than Tandem's 10, there are **no live voice or video calls** on any plan (practice is text plus voice, photo and video messages), and the community is much smaller than HelloTalk's or Tandem's. **Fluent** and **Polyglot** lift the new-conversation cap, raise translations to 300 and 1,000 a day, and add languages and filters; the [plans page](/pro) lists everything.

## Free to try, then earn or pay

- **Lingbe** gives you starter minutes of live calls with native speakers; after that you earn credit ("lingos") by taking calls in your own language, or pay for Lingbe Pro or lingo packs. See [LangX vs Lingbe](/open-source-alternative-to-lingbe).
- **Busuu** is a course app whose Community corrects your exercises. Free members can send a limited number of exercises and may wait longer; Premium removes that limit. See [LangX vs Busuu](/open-source-alternative-to-busuu).
- **italki**'s Community area (posts and questions that native speakers answer) is free, and Google Play does not list ads, but the main product is paid lessons and it no longer has a free partner search. See [LangX vs italki](/open-source-alternative-to-italki).

## Which free limits actually matter?

From most to least noticeable for a typical learner:

1. **Translation caps.** Beginners may want a translation on every other message. Tandem's 3 a day goes fast; LangX's 20 and HelloTalk's daily allowance last longer.
2. **New-conversation caps.** These matter in your first week, while you look for one or two good partners. After that you are mostly replying, which is unlimited on Tandem, HelloTalk, InterPals and LangX alike.
3. **Ads.** If they bother you, the ad-free free options here are LangX, and italki's Community for written answers only.
4. **Language count and filters.** Most free tiers allow one learning language, and gender or nearby search is paid on HelloTalk, Tandem and LangX.

## How to practice for free without hitting the limits

- **Spend new-conversation slots on good profiles.** Mention something from the profile and ask a real question; five thoughtful first messages beat twenty "hi"s. See [how to find a language exchange partner](/how-to-find-a-language-exchange-partner).
- **Save translation for whole sentences.** Look up single words in a dictionary instead.
- **Learn the most common words first**, so you need less translation. Our free [most common words lists](/tools/most-common-words) cover more than 50 languages.
- **Use two free apps together:** a big one to find people, a quieter one for the partner you keep.

## FAQ

### Is there a completely free language exchange app?

Yes. At the time of writing, Speaky has no paid tier in either app store, and InterPals says all its services are free. Both are paid for by ads. Conversation Exchange is also free to use, with an optional paid membership that removes ads and messaging limits.

### Which language exchange app has no ads?

LangX has no ads on any plan, including Free, and no advertising identifiers. It does use product analytics (PostHog, on its EU cloud, including a masked screen recording on iOS and Android), which you can switch off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) lists what is sent. HelloTalk, Tandem, Speaky on Android, InterPals, Slowly, Lingbe and Busuu all show ads on their free versions; HelloTalk VIP and Tandem Pro remove them.

### Is HelloTalk free?

Mostly. Chat, calls, Moments and Voicerooms are free. Translation, transliteration, voice-to-text and AI correction have daily caps, and the free version shows ads. VIP removes both.

### Is Tandem free?

Yes, with limits: 10 new conversations and 3 translations a day, up to an hour a day in Language Parties, and ads. Calls, voice messages and corrections are free. Tandem Pro lifts the caps and removes ads.

### Is LangX free?

Yes. The free plan has no ads and unlimited text, replies and corrections. It limits new conversations you start to 5 and translations to 20 per rolling 24 hours. Paid plans, Fluent and Polyglot, raise those limits; you can [get the app here](https://get.langx.io).

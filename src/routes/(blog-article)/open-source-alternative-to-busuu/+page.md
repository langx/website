If you want an open source alternative to Busuu for practicing with native speakers, LangX is a free, open source (BSD-3) language exchange app where you chat one-to-one with people who speak what you learn, and every message can be corrected. It is not a replacement for Busuu's structured course, so the honest answer to "LangX vs Busuu" is that LangX covers the talking-and-getting-corrected part, and for many learners the best setup is to use both.

This page compares the two as of September 2026. We make LangX, and we will be clear about where Busuu is the better tool.

## Busuu and LangX are different kinds of app

Busuu is a **course app**. It was founded in London in 2008 and has been owned by Chegg since 2022. It teaches 14 languages through lessons that run from A1 to C1 on the CEFR scale, with vocabulary review, grammar, and speaking and writing exercises. Its signature extra is **Community Corrections**: you submit a written or spoken exercise, and fluent speakers in the Busuu community correct it.

LangX is a **language exchange app**. There is no lesson path; what you can study alone is Echo, free spaced-repetition packs of phrases in six languages. You are matched with people who speak the language you are learning and are learning a language you speak, and you have real conversations, with corrections, translation and voice messages built into the chat.

So a Busuu alternative can mean two things:

1. **Another course app** (if you want structured lessons). LangX is not that.
2. **A better way to talk with and get corrected by native speakers** (if Busuu's community side is what you want more of). That is exactly what LangX is for.

## What Busuu does well

Busuu is a well-designed course app, and there is a lot to like:

- **A real curriculum.** Lessons follow the CEFR levels from A1 to C1, so you always know what comes next. For a true beginner, this structure is worth a lot.
- **Grammar and vocabulary review.** Busuu teaches grammar explicitly and brings vocabulary back for review, which a conversation app cannot do on its own.
- **Community Corrections.** Having a native speaker read what you wrote, or listen to what you said, and fix it is one of the most effective kinds of feedback.
- **A big community.** With a very large user base, popular languages have plenty of fluent speakers available to correct exercises.
- **Certificates.** Busuu offers level certificates for some languages, which some learners find motivating or useful.
- **A polished, low-pressure experience.** Short lessons fit into a commute.

## Where LangX differs

### Conversations, not exercises

Busuu's community feedback is attached to exercises: you answer a prompt, someone corrects it, and you may exchange a few replies. On LangX the conversation *is* the practice. You talk about whatever you and your partner care about, over days or weeks, and that is where you meet the vocabulary and phrasing that no course covers.

### Corrections are unlimited, for everyone

On LangX, your partner can hold any message and correct it, and corrections are unlimited on every plan, including Free. On Busuu, according to its own help pages, free members can send a limited number of exercises for correction, while Premium members get unlimited submissions and priority.

### Both sides benefit

Because matching on LangX runs in both directions, the person correcting you is also learning your language, and you correct them in return. That keeps the exchange balanced, because both of you get something out of every conversation.

### Translation and voice inside the chat

When you get stuck mid-conversation, LangX translates inside the chat. Voice messages let you practice pronunciation in context, and read-aloud lets you hear any message spoken.

### Open source and no ads

LangX's app and API are open source under BSD-3 on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx), you can host your own instance, and there are no ads on any plan. If you specifically want an open source alternative to Busuu's social side, that is the difference.

### What LangX does not do

- **No grammar explanations and no curriculum.** Echo's free phrase packs give you something to review, but if you need to be taught the basics, a course does that better.
- **No live calls,** only voice messages.
- **A smaller community,** so some language pairs have fewer people.
- **The free plan caps new conversations** at 5 per rolling 24 hours (replies and corrections are never capped). The [plans page](/plans) has details.

## Who should pick which?

**Pick Busuu if** you are a beginner, you want a structured path with grammar and vocabulary, and you like the idea of occasional native-speaker feedback on exercises.

**Pick LangX if** you already know the basics and want more real conversation, you want unlimited corrections without paying, or you care about open source and no ads.

**Use both if** you want steady progress. This is what we would recommend to most learners:

1. Do a Busuu lesson to learn a new structure or set of words.
2. Use it that same day in a LangX conversation.
3. Let your partner correct you, and note the fixes.

The course gives you the building blocks, and the exchange makes you use them with a real person. Our guide on [why consistent practice is key](/why-consistent-practice-is-key-to-language-learning-success) covers retention and real-world use in more detail.

## FAQ

### What is the best open source alternative to Busuu?

For practicing with and getting corrected by native speakers, LangX is an open source alternative to Busuu: the app and API are BSD-3 licensed on GitHub. It is not a course, so if you want structured lessons there is no direct open source equivalent of Busuu's curriculum.

### Is LangX a free Busuu alternative?

For the community side, yes. LangX's free plan includes unlimited replies and unlimited corrections, 5 new conversations and 20 translations per rolling 24 hours, and no ads. It does not include lessons.

### LangX vs Busuu: which is better for speaking practice?

For ongoing, real conversations, LangX, because it is built around one-to-one chats with voice messages. For guided speaking exercises within a course, Busuu. Neither has live calls built in.

### Can I use Busuu and LangX together?

Yes, and we recommend it. Learn new material in a Busuu lesson, then use it in a LangX conversation the same day and let your partner correct you.

### Are there apps like Busuu with unlimited free corrections?

LangX makes corrections unlimited on every plan. HelloTalk also has in-chat corrections. On Busuu, free members can send a limited number of exercises for correction.

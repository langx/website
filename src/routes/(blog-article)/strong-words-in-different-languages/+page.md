## Where these words come from

Each [/tools/say](/tools/say) page lists an English word in up to 53 languages. The words are ranked by how often they appear in film and TV subtitles (the OpenSubtitles 2018 frequency lists) and matched to English through Wiktionary, so every page shows where the word ranks in its language.

Three things to know before you copy anything:

- **Frequency lists count spellings, not meanings.** If a word for "brave" is spelled like an everyday phrase, it ranks very high for the wrong reason. We only print rows whose meaning matches, and we say where the full page's "most common" claim is one of these.
- **You get the form people say, not always the dictionary form.** Subtitles are speech, so a row can be "I promise" instead of "promise", or a noun in the case it takes inside a sentence. We mark the ones that matter.
- **The lists are lowercase.** We capitalize German nouns (*Schicksal*, *Rache*) the way you'd see them in a sentence.

## FAQ

### How do you say "strong" in different languages?

Dutch *sterk*, Swedish *stark*, Czech *silný*, Hungarian *erős*, Georgian ძლიერი, Hebrew חזק, Turkish *güçlü*, Indonesian and Malay *kuat* and Vietnamese *mạnh*. The [strong page](/tools/say/strong) has 48 languages, though some rows are false matches.

### How do you say "fate" in different languages?

Spanish *destino*, French *destin*, German *Schicksal*, Russian судьба, Persian سرنوشت, Urdu قسمت, Turkish *kader* and Indonesian *takdir*. The [fate page](/tools/say/fate) has 32 languages.

### How do you say "war" in other languages?

Spanish, Italian and Portuguese *guerra*, French *guerre*, Russian война, Hebrew מלחמה, Persian and Urdu جنگ, Hindi युद्ध, Turkish *savaş* and Korean 전쟁. The [war page](/tools/say/war) has 50 languages.

### Is faith the same word as trust or belief?

Often, yes. Hindi विश्वास and Bengali বিশ্বাস cover faith, belief and trust, and Hebrew אמון, Italian *fiducia* and Tagalog *tiwala* appear on both the faith and trust pages. Words like Persian ایمان are closer to religious faith.

### Can I use these words for a tattoo?

Yes, but check first. Some rows are verb forms, case forms or plurals, and some have a second meaning. Ask a native speaker, ideally two, before you commit.

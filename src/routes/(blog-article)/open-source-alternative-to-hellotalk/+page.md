LangX is an open source alternative to HelloTalk: a language exchange app with public code (BSD-3), two-way matching and corrections that are unlimited on every plan. HelloTalk is far bigger and does much more, with Moments, Voicerooms, livestreams and calls, so it remains the better pick if you want a busy social network for learners rather than a focused one-to-one exchange.

Everything about HelloTalk below comes from its app store listings and its own site at the time of writing (September 2026).

## What HelloTalk does well

HelloTalk is one of the best-known language exchange apps for good reasons, and any honest HelloTalk alternative page should start there.

- **Sheer size.** HelloTalk calls itself "the world's leading language exchange app", and Google Play lists more than ten million downloads. For almost any language pair, somebody is online. LangX cannot match that today.
- **Moments.** A global feed where learners post about language, daily life, travel and culture. You can post a short text or voice clip and several native speakers can correct the same sentence. It is a genuinely good way to get feedback without committing to a conversation.
- **Voicerooms.** Live group voice chats on themes like daily life, travel or exam prep, running around the clock. Great for listening practice and for shy speakers who want to warm up before talking.
- **HelloTalk Live.** Hosts stream lessons and cultural talks, and viewers can join the stage to speak.
- **Strong chat tools.** Translation, transliteration (very handy for scripts like Japanese, Korean or Chinese), correction tools and text-to-speech are all inside the chat, plus voice and video calls.
- **Wide language coverage.** HelloTalk lists more than 260 languages.
- **A generous free tier.** HelloTalk's own guide says chat, voice messages, Moments, corrections, Voicerooms and livestreams are free, with daily caps on translation, AI correction and voice-to-text. VIP adds unlimited use of those tools, more new partners a day, Nearby and city search, a gender filter and no ads.

If you want a lively, social place to learn with lots of ways to join in, HelloTalk is hard to beat.

## Where LangX differs from HelloTalk

LangX is deliberately smaller in scope. It is built around one-to-one language exchange and makes different trade-offs.

### An open source alternative to HelloTalk

The LangX app and API are public on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx) under the BSD-3 license. You can read the code, see exactly what the app sends, contribute, or host your own instance. HelloTalk is closed source. If transparency matters to you, that is the main reason to look for an open source alternative to HelloTalk.

### Two-way matching at the center

LangX has no live rooms or livestreams. Its Feed is narrower than Moments: you post a sentence for the community to correct, or ask how something is pronounced and get answers as recordings. The center of the app is Discover, which only shows people who speak the language you are learning *and* are learning a language you speak. Every chat starts balanced. If you are new to the idea, [what is a language exchange](/what-is-a-language-exchange) explains why that balance matters.

### Corrections and replies are unlimited everywhere

On LangX you hold any message to correct it, and corrections are unlimited on every plan, Free included. So are replies to anyone who writes to you. The free plan limits new conversations you start (5 a day) and translations (20 a day), never how much you talk or teach. The full breakdown is on the [plans page](/pro).

### No ads

LangX shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. It does have product analytics (PostHog on its EU cloud, including a masked screen recording on iOS and Android where every word and image is replaced by a grey block on the device). It is on by default and switches off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) lists exactly what is sent.

### Streaks and tokens instead of livestreams

Instead of livestreams and rooms, LangX keeps you coming back with daily streaks, leaderboards and the [LangX Token](/tokens), an in-app point you earn by messaging and correcting others. It cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn.

## Is LangX a free HelloTalk alternative?

Yes, if "free" means a usable free plan rather than a trial. LangX Free includes unlimited text messages, unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day. Fluent and Polyglot add unlimited new conversations, more translations, more languages on your profile, extra filters, and features like "who viewed you" and incognito. Prices are set per region and shown in the app.

HelloTalk also has a generous free tier, so this is not a reason to switch by itself. The real differences are open source, two-way matching, no ads, and focus.

## LangX vs HelloTalk: who should pick which?

**Pick HelloTalk if you:**

- enjoy social features: posting on Moments, joining Voicerooms, watching livestreams
- want voice or video calls inside the app
- learn a less common language and need the largest possible community
- rely on transliteration for a new script

**Pick LangX if you:**

- want an open source alternative to HelloTalk you can audit or self-host
- prefer a quiet one-to-one exchange over a busy social network
- want every partner you see to be a two-way match
- want unlimited corrections and no ads on the free plan

**Or combine them.** Some learners use HelloTalk's Moments for quick feedback and a smaller app for steady one-to-one partners. ## FAQ

### What is the best open source alternative to HelloTalk?

LangX is an open source alternative to HelloTalk. Its app and API are public under the BSD-3 license and can be self-hosted. It focuses on one-to-one exchanges with two-way matching and unlimited corrections, rather than feeds and live rooms.

### LangX vs HelloTalk: which is bigger?

HelloTalk, by far. It has a much larger community and many more features, including Moments, Voicerooms, livestreams and calls. LangX is smaller and more focused.

### Is there a free HelloTalk alternative with no ads?

LangX's Free plan has no ads and includes unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day. Paid plans raise those limits.

### Does LangX have something like HelloTalk Moments or Voicerooms?

Partly. LangX has a Feed where you can post a sentence for others to correct or ask for pronunciation help, which covers the feedback side of Moments. It has no Voicerooms, livestreams or live calls; conversations are one-to-one, with voice, photo and video messages.

### What are other apps like HelloTalk?

Tandem, Speaky and ConversationExchange are well-known apps like HelloTalk, and italki suits learners who want paid tutors. The [compare page](/compare) lists every LangX comparison.

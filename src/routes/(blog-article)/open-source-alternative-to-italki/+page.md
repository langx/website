If you want an open source alternative to italki for free conversation practice with native speakers, LangX is a free, open source (BSD-3) language exchange app that matches you with people who speak what you learn and learn what you speak, with corrections on every message. It does not replace an italki teacher: italki is mainly a marketplace for paid one-to-one lessons, and LangX is for the free peer practice you do between those lessons.

This page compares LangX vs italki as of September 2026. We make LangX, and we will be honest about where a paid teacher is simply the better choice.

## What italki is today

italki was founded in 2007 as an online language exchange community and is headquartered in Hong Kong. Over the years it became best known for its **teacher marketplace**: you book paid one-to-one video lessons with professional teachers or community tutors, who set their own prices and schedules. It also offers group classes and a paid italki Plus subscription with study materials.

It still has a **free Community**, where you can post writing for corrections, ask language questions, take quizzes and read posts from teachers and learners.

What it no longer has is a dedicated **language partner search**. italki used to let you look for free exchange partners, and many learners joined for exactly that. Posts on italki's own community show users asking where the feature went and being told it is no longer available, going back to at least early 2021. italki has not, as far as we can find, published an official date. If you searched for an "italki language partner" and could not find the option, this is why.

## What italki does well

italki is very good at what it has become, and for many goals it is the right tool:

- **Real teachers.** A professional teacher can diagnose your mistakes, plan a path, explain grammar and push you in a way a peer usually cannot. If you are preparing for an exam, a job interview or a move abroad, this matters.
- **Huge choice.** Teachers for well over 100 languages, including many that are hard to find anywhere else, at a range of prices, with reviews and trial lessons.
- **Flexibility.** Book when you want, with whom you want, for as long as you want. No fixed course.
- **Community tutors for conversation.** If you want live speaking practice with someone whose job is to keep the conversation going, italki's community tutors are designed for exactly that.
- **A free community.** Posting a short text and having native speakers correct it is useful, and costs nothing.

If you can afford regular lessons and want fast, guided progress, italki is an excellent choice. LangX is not trying to replace that.

## Where LangX differs

### Free practice with peers, not paid lessons

On LangX nobody is paid. You help someone with your native language and they help you with theirs. That makes it possible to practice every day, not just when you have booked a lesson.

### A partner search that still exists

LangX is built entirely around what italki removed: finding language exchange partners. Matching runs in both directions, so you only see people who speak the language you are learning *and* are learning a language you speak. Free filters cover country, age and level.

### Corrections in every conversation

Your partner can hold any message and correct it, and corrections are unlimited on every plan, including Free. On italki's community you get corrections on posts; on LangX you get them inside an ongoing conversation.

### Translation and voice in the chat

LangX translates inside the chat when you are stuck, supports voice messages for pronunciation practice, and reads messages aloud.

### Open source and no ads

LangX's app and API are open source under BSD-3 on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx), and you can host your own instance. There are no ads on any plan.

### What LangX will not do for you

- **It is not a teacher.** Your partner is a native speaker, not a trained instructor. They can tell you what sounds natural, but not always why.
- **No live calls,** only voice messages. italki's lessons are live video.
- **No curriculum or lesson plans.**
- **A smaller community,** so rare languages may have few people.
- **The free plan caps new conversations** at 5 per rolling 24 hours; replies and corrections are never capped. See the [plans page](/pro).

## Who should pick which?

**Pick italki if** you want a professional teacher, need structured or exam-focused help, or want guaranteed live speaking time with someone paid to help you.

**Pick LangX if** you want a free italki alternative for the language exchange side, the part italki's partner search used to cover, or you want daily practice without paying per session.

**Use both if** you take italki lessons. This is the combination we would recommend:

1. In your italki lesson, learn and practice something new with your teacher.
2. Between lessons, use it in LangX conversations every day.
3. Bring the mistakes your LangX partners corrected to your next lesson and ask your teacher *why*.

Lessons give you expert guidance; daily exchange gives you the repetition that lessons alone cannot. Our post on [why consistent practice is key](/why-consistent-practice-is-key-to-language-learning-success) goes into more detail.

## FAQ

### What is the best open source alternative to italki?

For free practice with native speakers, LangX is an open source alternative to italki: the app and API are BSD-3 licensed on GitHub. There is no open source equivalent of italki's paid teacher marketplace.

### Does italki still have language partners?

Not as a dedicated feature. italki's language partner search was removed; users on italki's own community were already noting it was gone in early 2021. The free Community, for writing corrections and questions, is still there.

### Is LangX a free italki alternative?

For peer practice, yes. LangX's free plan has unlimited replies and unlimited corrections, 5 new conversations and 20 translations per rolling 24 hours, and no ads. It does not offer lessons with teachers.

### LangX vs italki: which should a beginner use?

Many beginners progress fastest with some guided lessons, which italki provides. Adding LangX gives you free daily practice between lessons. A beginner who cannot pay for lessons can still use LangX, ideally alongside a course.

### Are there apps like italki with free video calls?

Tandem and HelloTalk offer free calls with exchange partners, and Lingbe connects you to live voice calls. LangX has voice messages but no live calls.

If you want an open source alternative to Memrise for speaking with real people, LangX is a free, open source (BSD-3) language exchange app that matches you with native speakers who can correct any message you send. Memrise is still the better tool for learning vocabulary with native-speaker videos, spaced review and AI practice, so the honest answer to "LangX vs Memrise" is: Memrise helps you learn the words, LangX gives you people to use them with.

We make LangX, so keep that in mind. Memrise has changed a lot over the years, and everything about it below comes from its own site, blog and app store listings at the time of writing (September 2026).

## What is Memrise in 2026?

Many people remember [Memrise](https://www.memrise.com) as a flashcard site full of user-made courses and funny "mems." It has changed a lot since then:

- **2010:** Memrise launches in London, founded by memory champion Ed Cooke, Ben Whately and neuroscientist Greg Detre. It becomes known for spaced repetition, mnemonic "mems" and a huge library of community courses.
- **2022:** Memrise phases out user-made mems and launches **MemBot**, an AI chat partner for spoken and written practice.
- **2024:** Community courses move out of the app to a separate website. The app now centers on one official course per language, organized into real-life **Scenarios** with Learn and Review sections.
- **November 2025:** Memrise says it is not shutting down community courses and is exploring how to bring community content back into the main product.
- **February 2026:** Memrise adds Exam Prep (IELTS, GCSE, A-Level, IB and CEFR-aligned practice with an AI examiner), Memrise Stories, and **Podchats**, unscripted AI voice conversations with an AI podcast host for intermediate and advanced English learners.
- **May 2026:** Mems return, now with AI able to generate the memory image for you.

So today Memrise is best described as an **official-course app built on native-speaker videos, spaced review and AI conversation practice**. Community courses still exist, on their own site.

## What Memrise does well

Memrise has been around since 2010 for good reasons, and a fair Memrise alternative page should say so.

- **Native-speaker videos.** Seeing and hearing real locals say a phrase, with their own accent and body language, is one of the best things any app offers. Memrise has a very large library of them.
- **Spaced repetition done well.** Memrise was built by memory experts, and its review system is still one of the most effective ways to make vocabulary stick.
- **Mems are back.** Visual memory tricks, now with AI-generated images, make hard words memorable.
- **Practical, scenario-based content.** Official courses focus on things you will actually say: ordering food, introducing yourself, making plans.
- **AI practice with low pressure.** MemBot and Podchats let you speak and write without worrying about a real person's reaction, and Podchats gives you a transcript and feedback afterward.
- **Exam prep.** IELTS, GCSE, A-Level, IB and CEFR-aligned practice with a projected score is rare in a consumer app.
- **A huge long tail.** Between official courses and community courses, Memrise covers well over a hundred languages.

If your main problem is vocabulary, Memrise remains one of the strongest apps you can use.

## Where LangX differs from Memrise

### Real conversation, not just recognition

Knowing a word when you see it and using it in a live conversation are different skills. Memrise builds the first; LangX is for the second. On LangX, every profile in Discover speaks the language you are learning and is learning a language you speak, so you get a balanced exchange with a real person who uses the words you studied in ways no course anticipated.

### Human corrections, unlimited on every plan

On LangX, your partner taps any message to correct it, and corrections are unlimited on every plan, Free included. A native speaker tells you what sounds natural, not only what is technically correct, and you return the favor in your own language.

### Open source and a real free plan

The LangX app and API are public on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx) under the BSD-3 license and can be self-hosted. Memrise is closed source, and many former users were frustrated when community courses were moved out of the app, which is part of why people look for an open source alternative to Memrise. LangX Free includes unlimited text messages, unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations and 20 translations per rolling 24 hours. See [plans](/pro) for more.

### No ads

LangX shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. It does run product analytics (PostHog on its EU cloud, including a masked screen recording on iOS and Android where every word and image becomes a grey block on the device). It is on by default and switches off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) has the full list.

### What LangX does not do

- **No flashcards, spaced repetition or course.** LangX will not drill vocabulary for you.
- **No community course library,** so it is not a replacement for Memrise's user-made courses.
- **No AI chat today.** LangX Copilot, private AI feedback, is planned for Polyglot but not shipped.
- **No live calls,** only voice messages, and a smaller community means fewer partners for some language pairs.

## FAQ

### What is the best open source alternative to Memrise?

For speaking practice with native speakers, LangX is an open source alternative to Memrise: its app and API are BSD-3 licensed on GitHub. For flashcards, an open source spaced-repetition tool is the closer match; LangX does not do vocabulary drills.

### What happened to Memrise community courses?

In 2024, Memrise moved community courses out of the mobile app to a separate website. In November 2025, it said it was not shutting them down and was exploring how to bring community content back into the main product.

### Is there a free Memrise alternative with no ads?

LangX's Free plan has no ads and includes unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day. It is for conversation, not flashcards. Google Play lists Memrise as containing ads.

### LangX vs Memrise: which is better for speaking?

Memrise offers AI speaking practice and native-speaker videos to imitate. LangX gives you real conversations with native speakers and their corrections. For speaking with confidence, most learners benefit from both.

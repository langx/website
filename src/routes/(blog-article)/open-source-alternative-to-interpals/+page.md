LangX is an open source alternative to InterPals built only for language exchange: a free app with public code (BSD-3), two-way matching and corrections on any message, unlimited on every plan. InterPals is older, far larger and broader, a pen-pal community for friendship, travel and culture as well as languages, so it remains the better pick if you want to meet all kinds of people rather than only exchange partners.

Everything about InterPals below comes from its own site, its FAQ and its app store listings at the time of writing (September 2026).

## What InterPals does well

InterPals has been around since 1998, which makes it one of the oldest pen-pal communities on the web. A fair InterPals alternative page has to give it credit.

- **Longevity and scale.** More than a quarter of a century of members in a huge number of countries. InterPals says it has 8.5 million members, and whatever language you are learning, there is a good chance somebody speaks it.
- **Genuinely free.** Its FAQ says every service is free and the site is funded by advertising. Creating a profile, searching and messaging cost nothing.
- **Good search.** You can search by country, city, age and the languages someone speaks or is learning, and see who is online right now. There is no swiping, just real profiles you browse.
- **More than language.** Groups around shared interests, a community feed, photo albums and comments. You can also meet travelers who are visiting your area. For cultural exchange, that breadth is a strength.
- **Message translation.** The Android listing mentions built-in message translation for when you get stuck.
- **Moderation and control.** Message requests let you decide who gets through, and a moderation team reviews reported profiles, photos and messages. InterPals is clear that it is not a dating site.
- **Long friendships.** Plenty of members have written to the same pen pal for years. If what you want is a long correspondence rather than quick practice, that culture is valuable.

## Where LangX differs from InterPals

LangX is narrower on purpose. It exists for one job, practicing a language with a native speaker who is learning yours, and every feature serves that job.

### An open source alternative to InterPals

The LangX app and API are public on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx) under the BSD-3 license. You can read the code, see what the app sends, contribute, or host your own instance. InterPals is closed source. If transparency matters to you, that is the main reason to look for an open source alternative to InterPals.

### Everyone you see is a two-way match

On InterPals you can filter by language, but you are browsing a general community: many members are there for friendship or travel, and you often have to say clearly that you want a language exchange. LangX's Discover only shows people who speak the language you are learning *and* are learning a language you speak, so every chat starts with both people wanting the same thing. Our guide on [how to find a language exchange partner](/how-to-find-a-language-exchange-partner) covers what to look for either way.

### Corrections built into the chat

InterPals does not list a correction tool, so a partner who wants to fix your sentence has to retype it. On LangX you tap any message to correct it, and the correction appears right there in the conversation. Corrections are unlimited on every plan, Free included, and so are replies to anyone who writes to you. That is the part that teaches, and it is never rationed.

### Learning tools around the conversation

LangX adds the things a learner reaches for mid-conversation: translation inside the chat, read-aloud so you hear how a message sounds, voice and photo messages, daily streaks, leaderboards and the [LangX Token](/tokens), an in-app point you earn by messaging and correcting others. It cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn.

### Similar limits on new contacts, different reasons

Both apps limit how many new people you can contact per day. InterPals does it to fight spam, with the limit rising the longer you are a member, and places no limit on messages with people you already write to. LangX Free allows 5 new conversations a day, with unlimited replies and corrections; the paid plans remove that limit.

### No ads

LangX shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. It does have product analytics (PostHog on its EU cloud, including a masked screen recording on iOS and Android where every word and image is replaced by a grey block on the device). It is on by default and switches off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) lists exactly what is sent.

## Is LangX a free InterPals alternative?

Yes. LangX Free includes unlimited text messages, unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day, with no ads. Fluent and Polyglot add unlimited new conversations, more translations, more languages on your profile, extra filters, and features like "who viewed you" and incognito; see the [plans page](/pro). Prices are set per region and shown in the app.

InterPals is free too, and its FAQ mentions no subscription. So the choice comes down to what you want from the people you meet: a broad pen-pal community, or a focused language exchange with tools for learning.

## LangX vs InterPals: who should pick which?

**Pick InterPals if you:**

- want pen pals and friends first, with language practice as one part of it
- enjoy long, slower messages and years-long correspondences
- want groups, photo albums and a community feed
- are traveling and want to meet locals or other travelers
- want no subscription at all and do not mind ads

**Pick LangX if you:**

- want an open source alternative to InterPals you can audit or self-host
- want every person you see to be a two-way language match
- want corrections, translation and read-aloud inside the chat
- want no ads on the free plan

**Or use both.** An InterPals pen pal for long letters and a LangX partner for daily corrected practice work well together. ## FAQ

### What is the best open source alternative to InterPals?

LangX is an open source alternative to InterPals focused on language exchange. Its app and API are public under the BSD-3 license and can be self-hosted. It offers two-way matching and unlimited corrections rather than a general pen-pal community.

### LangX vs InterPals: which is better for learning a language?

For focused practice, LangX, because every match wants to learn your language and corrections, translation and read-aloud are built into the chat. For meeting a wide range of people, cultural exchange and long pen-pal friendships, InterPals has the bigger and broader community.

### Is there a free InterPals alternative without ads?

LangX's Free plan has no ads and includes unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day. InterPals is free as well but, by its own description, funded by advertising.

### Is InterPals good for language exchange?

It can be. You can search by the languages people speak and learn, and many members practice languages. It has no correction tools, and because it is a general pen-pal site you may need to say clearly that you are looking for a language exchange.

### What are other sites like InterPals?

Slowly and ConversationExchange are the closest sites and apps like InterPals for pen-pal style exchange, while HelloTalk, Tandem, Speaky and LangX are chat-based language exchange apps. The [compare page](/compare) lists every LangX comparison.

LangX is an open source, social alternative to Duolingo for the part Duolingo does not cover: real conversation with real people. Think of it as Duolingo made of people instead of lessons. It is a free language exchange app with public code (BSD-3) that matches you with native speakers who are learning your language, with corrections on any message, unlimited on every plan. It is not a course, so if you want structured bite-size lessons, Duolingo is still the better tool, and many learners get the most out of using both.

This page is a head-to-head LangX vs Duolingo comparison, checked at the time of writing (September 2026). We make LangX, and we will say plainly where Duolingo wins.

## Duolingo and LangX are different kinds of app

**Duolingo** is a course app. It teaches 40+ languages, plus Math, Music and Chess, through short, game-like lessons with reading, listening, writing and speaking exercises. Streaks, leaderboards and a friendly owl keep you coming back. Every language course is free, with paid Super and Max plans on top.

**LangX** is a language exchange app. There is no lesson path — what it has to study alone is Echo, free spaced-repetition packs of phrases in six languages, plus a card for any message a partner corrects. You are matched with someone who speaks the language you are learning and is learning a language you speak, and you have real conversations with corrections, translation and voice messages built into the chat.

So when people search for a Duolingo alternative, they usually mean one of two things:

1. **Another course**, ideally one that is open source. LangX is not that; see the section on open source course projects below.
2. **Something that finally gets them talking to people.** That is exactly what LangX is for.

## What Duolingo does well

Duolingo is very good at its job, and an honest Duolingo alternative page should say so.

- **It gets you to show up.** Streaks, leagues and five-minute lessons make daily practice feel easy. For most learners, consistency is the hardest part, and Duolingo is excellent at it.
- **It is a gentle on-ramp.** You can start a language from zero, with no plan and no textbook, and be reading simple sentences within days.
- **Every language course is free.** Duolingo's own listing says so. That makes it a low-risk way to find out whether you like a language.
- **Lots of languages, and more than languages.** 40+ language courses, from Spanish and Japanese to Irish and Hawaiian, plus Math, Music and Chess.
- **It has started to address speaking.** The Max plan includes Video Call with Lily, an AI character you talk to, and Roleplay scenarios with AI feedback.

## Where LangX differs from Duolingo

### Real people instead of a lesson path

Duolingo mostly asks you to recognize, arrange or complete sentences someone else wrote. A conversation asks you to build sentences from nothing, in real time, and to understand whatever the other person says back, including slang and questions you did not see coming. On LangX, every chat is with a person: someone who speaks the language you are learning *and* is learning a language you speak, so you help each other. If the idea is new, start with [what is a language exchange](/what-is-a-language-exchange).

### Corrections from a native speaker

Duolingo marks your answer right or wrong against its own model answers. On LangX a native speaker holds your message and shows you how they would actually say it. Corrections are unlimited on every plan, Free included, and so are replies to anyone who writes to you. That is the part that teaches, and it is never rationed.

### An open source alternative to Duolingo

The LangX app and API are public on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx) under the BSD-3 license. You can read the code, see what the app sends, contribute, or host your own instance. Duolingo is closed source. If transparency matters to you, LangX is an open source alternative to Duolingo for conversation practice, though not for lessons.

### Familiar habits, different content

If you like Duolingo's streak, you will feel at home: LangX has daily streaks, leaderboards and the [LangX Token](/tokens), an in-app point you earn by messaging and correcting others. It cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn. The difference is what the streak is for: a conversation instead of a lesson. Our post on [staying motivated](/how-to-stay-motivated-while-learning-a-new-language) has more on building the habit.

### No ads

LangX shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. It does have product analytics (PostHog on its EU cloud, including a masked screen recording on iOS and Android where every word and image is replaced by a grey block on the device). It is on by default and switches off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) lists exactly what is sent.

### What LangX does not do

LangX does not teach you a language from zero. There is no lesson path, no grammar course and no placement test; Echo's free phrase packs give you something to review from day one, but they will not explain the grammar. It works best once you can write simple sentences, which is roughly where Duolingo gets you. And there are no live calls; conversations are text, voice, photo and video messages.

## Is there an open source Duolingo alternative for lessons?

If you want an open source course rather than conversation practice, the options are thinner than you might hope. We checked the best-known ones in September 2026:

- **LibreLingo** was the most prominent open source Duolingo-style project. Its original GitHub repository has been archived and is read-only, with a note from its creator saying it is no longer maintained, and the librelingo.app site no longer loads. A community fork, **LibreLingoRelive**, is still being developed on Codeberg (it had new commits in August 2026) under the AGPL-3.0 license. It is a project for tinkerers and contributors more than a polished app today.
- **Anki** is open source and actively maintained. It is a flashcard app rather than a course, but paired with a textbook and a conversation partner it covers a lot of what people use Duolingo for.

For a structured course, closed-source apps like Busuu and Babbel are the realistic Duolingo alternatives. Our [Duolingo alternatives for speaking](/duolingo-alternatives-for-speaking) guide compares them by goal.

## Is LangX a free Duolingo alternative?

For conversation, yes. LangX Free includes unlimited text messages, unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day, with no ads. Fluent and Polyglot add unlimited new conversations, more translations and more languages; see the [plans page](/pro). Prices are set per region and shown in the app.

Duolingo's free version is generous too. The difference is not price; it is what you get: lessons on Duolingo, people on LangX.

## LangX vs Duolingo: who should pick which?

**Pick Duolingo if you:**

- are starting a language from zero
- want short, structured lessons and a clear path
- mainly want a daily habit and a gentle introduction

**Pick LangX if you:**

- can already write simple sentences and want to use them
- want corrections from native speakers, not an answer key
- want an open source alternative to Duolingo you can audit or self-host
- want practice with no ads on the free plan

**Or use both**, which is what we would suggest for most learners. Do your Duolingo lesson, then write two or three sentences to a LangX partner using what you just learned and ask them to correct you. A little of each every day tends to beat a long session of either once a week. To fill the gaps in between, try the [most common words](/tools/most-common-words) in your language, the [word game](/tools/word-game) and [how to say everyday words](/tools/say) in other languages.

## FAQ

### Is there an open source alternative to Duolingo?

For conversation, LangX is an open source alternative to Duolingo: a language exchange app, public under the BSD-3 license. For lessons, the best-known open source project, LibreLingo, is archived, though a community fork called LibreLingoRelive continues on Codeberg. Anki is an open source option for vocabulary.

### LangX vs Duolingo: which is better?

They do different jobs. Duolingo is better for starting from zero with structured lessons. LangX is better for real conversations and corrections from native speakers. Most learners benefit from both.

### What is the best free Duolingo alternative for speaking?

A language exchange app, where you practice with real people for free. LangX's Free plan has no ads and includes unlimited replies and corrections. Our [Duolingo alternatives for speaking](/duolingo-alternatives-for-speaking) guide covers the other options.

### Can I use LangX and Duolingo together?

Yes, and it works well: Duolingo for new words and grammar, LangX to use them with a person who corrects you.

### What are other apps like Duolingo?

Busuu and Babbel are the closest apps like Duolingo for structured lessons. For speaking, language exchange apps such as LangX and Tandem, or tutors on italki and Preply. The [compare page](/compare) lists every LangX comparison.

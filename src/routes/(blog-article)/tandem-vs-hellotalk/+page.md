**HelloTalk is better if you want the biggest community and many ways to practice at once: chat, a social feed, group voice rooms and livestreams. Tandem is better if you want a calmer, reviewed community built around one-to-one exchange with a steady partner.** Both are free to start, both show ads on the free tier, and both put their most useful extras, like unlimited translation, behind a subscription.

Below is a feature-by-feature comparison, checked against each company's own site, help pages and store listings in September 2026. We make a third app, LangX, so we mention it once at the end and otherwise keep out of the way.

## Signing up: review vs instant access

This is the first real difference you will notice.

**[Tandem](https://tandem.net) reviews every application.** You create a profile, and a person checks it before you can message anyone. Tandem's FAQ says it rejects profiles without a real photo or name, and applicants under 16, and that there is occasionally a waitlist in certain countries to keep the community and its languages balanced. The upside is a community that feels more deliberate. The downside is that you may wait before your first conversation, and some people wait a long time.

**[HelloTalk](https://www.hellotalk.com) lets you in straight away.** You pick your languages and start browsing partners within minutes. That makes it the better choice if you want to practice tonight, at the cost of a community that is less filtered at the door.

## Community and ways to practice

Both apps have communities that are very large by any standard, many times bigger than smaller apps like ours. The difference is shape, not just size.

**HelloTalk works like a language-learning social network.** Alongside one-to-one chat you get:

- **Moments**, a public feed where you post a few sentences and native speakers correct them. It is one of the fastest ways to get several people's feedback on the same text.
- **Voicerooms**, live group voice chats on set topics, where you can listen first and speak when ready.
- **HelloTalk Live**, where hosts stream lessons or daily life and you can ask to join the stage.

**Tandem is built around the one-to-one exchange.** Its Community tab is a list of profiles you can filter, and the aim is to find a partner and talk. It has group features too:

- **Language Parties**, live audio rooms similar to Voicerooms. Free members can join for up to 60 minutes a day.
- **Language Clubs**, topic groups for posts and questions.

If you enjoy scrolling, posting and dipping into rooms, HelloTalk will feel alive. If you find feeds distracting and want one or two good partners, Tandem's structure helps.

## Corrections, translation and learning tools

Both apps let your partner correct a message inside the chat, showing the fix next to what you wrote. Both have translation built in. Both have added AI tools. The free tiers are where they differ.

**Tandem's free plan** includes inline corrections, audio messages, calls, and a small daily translation allowance; Tandem's own overview lists three free translations a day. **Tandem Pro** adds unlimited translations, an AI toolkit (grammar check, a word finder, conversation-starter suggestions, rewrite, and explanations of corrections), unlimited transcriptions and no ads.

**HelloTalk's free tier** includes its correction and transliteration tools, with daily caps on translation, AI correction and voice-to-text. When you hit the translation cap, the tool is unavailable until the next day. **HelloTalk VIP** removes those caps and the ads.

In practice, HelloTalk's free tier feels a little more generous for day-to-day chatting, while Tandem Pro's AI toolkit is the more complete paid package if you want explanations and not just fixes.

## Voice and video

Both support voice messages and live voice and video calls with a partner. For group speaking practice, HelloTalk's Voicerooms and Tandem's Language Parties are roughly equivalent, with two differences: HelloTalk also has livestreams, and Tandem limits free members to an hour of Parties a day. HelloTalk VIP adds live subtitles in Voicerooms, which helps if you are still training your ear.

## Finding people near you

Both apps can find members nearby, which is useful if you want to meet in person or you are about to travel. On both it is a paid feature: **Nearby and Travel filters** in Tandem Pro, and **Nearby search** in HelloTalk VIP. Tandem's Travel filter goes one step further and lets you look up members in a city you are about to visit.

## Free vs paid: what you actually get without paying

You can practice seriously on either app without paying. Both free tiers include text and voice chat with partners, corrections, calls and group audio. What you give up:

- **On Tandem:** more translations, the AI toolkit, location filters, seeing who visited your profile, more daily conversations (Tandem's overview lists 10 a day free, 30 with Pro), and ad-free use.
- **On HelloTalk:** unlimited translation, AI correction and voice-to-text, location and gender search, more new partner contacts a day, Voiceroom subtitles, and ad-free use.

Prices vary by country and both apps change their plans often, so compare the current offer in your own app store before subscribing.

## Safety and moderation

Both apps have reporting and blocking, and both communities contain a minority of people who are not there to learn languages. Tandem's profile review screens some of them out before they can message anyone, and its Community Safety team reviews reports, and the reported member is not notified that you reported them. HelloTalk's instant signup means more of that filtering happens after the fact, through reports and blocks.

On either app: keep conversations in the app until you trust someone, do not share personal details early, and treat requests for money or moves to other platforms as red flags.

## Verdict: which is better for you?

- **Choose HelloTalk if** you want to start today, like a social feed, want quick corrections from many people through Moments, or learn a popular language where a huge, active community means fast replies.
- **Choose Tandem if** you want a more curated community, prefer one steady partner, find social feeds distracting, or want the AI explanation tools in Pro.
- **Choose HelloTalk if** you want the most generous free tier for daily chatting.
- **Choose Tandem if** you learn a sign language, or you want to find partners in a city you are about to visit.
- **Use both if** you can. Plenty of learners find partners on HelloTalk and have their longer conversations on Tandem, or the other way round.

## FAQ

### Is Tandem or HelloTalk better for beginners?

HelloTalk is usually easier for beginners because you can start immediately and use transliteration and translation in the chat, and Moments lets you post short texts for correction without holding a conversation. Tandem works well for beginners too, once your application is approved.

### Is HelloTalk free?

Yes. HelloTalk's core features, including text and voice chat, Moments and Voicerooms, are free. The free tier has ads and daily caps on translation, AI correction and voice-to-text; VIP removes them.

### Is Tandem free?

Yes. Tandem is free to join and use for chat, corrections, audio messages and calls, with daily limits on translations and new conversations, and ads. Tandem Pro removes the limits and adds AI tools and location filters.

### Why is my Tandem application taking so long?

Tandem reviews every application individually, and says there is occasionally a waitlist in some countries to keep languages balanced. Check that your profile has a real photo and name, since those are common reasons for rejection.

If you want an open source alternative to Speak that gets you talking with real people instead of an AI tutor, LangX is a free, open source (BSD-3) language exchange app that matches you with native speakers and lets them correct any message you send. Speak is the better tool for structured, out-loud speaking drills with instant AI feedback, so the honest answer to "LangX vs Speak" is that they do different jobs, and many learners are best served by using both.

We make LangX, so we have a stake in this. Everything about Speak below comes from its own site, its app store listings and reputable reviews at the time of writing (September 2026).

## Speak and LangX are different kinds of app

[Speak](https://www.speak.com) is an **AI speaking tutor**. It was founded in 2016 in San Francisco by Speakeasy Labs, is backed by the OpenAI Startup Fund, and is built around what it calls the Speak Method: learn phrases native speakers actually use, practice them until they are automatic, then apply them in back-and-forth conversation with the Speak Tutor AI. You speak out loud the whole time, and the app gives real-time feedback on pronunciation and phrasing.

LangX is a **language exchange app**. There are no lessons and no AI tutor today. You are matched with people who speak the language you are learning and are learning a language you speak, and you have real conversations with them, with corrections, translation and voice messages built into the chat.

So a "Speak alternative" can mean two things:

1. **Another AI tutor** (if you like talking to an AI). LangX is not that.
2. **A way to practice with real humans** once the AI has done its job of getting you started. That is exactly what LangX is for.

## What Speak does well

Speak is one of the most polished AI language apps around, and any fair Speak alternative page has to start with what it gets right.

- **It makes you talk from day one.** Most apps let you tap and swipe for months. Speak has you saying full sentences out loud in the first session, which is the habit most learners never build.
- **No social pressure.** You can make the same mistake twenty times and the AI will not sigh. For nervous beginners, that matters a lot.
- **Instant feedback.** Speech recognition and AI feedback on pronunciation and phrasing arrive while you are still speaking, not days later.
- **Available at 3 a.m.** There is always a conversation partner. No waiting for a reply, no time zones.
- **A real curriculum.** Units are built from tutor lessons, speaking drills, vocab builders, roleplays and Q&A with the tutor, so beginners always know what to do next. Reviewers consistently call it one of the strongest choices for structured beginner speaking practice.
- **Roleplays and free talk.** Rehearse ordering food, a job interview or small talk, then switch to open conversation with the AI.
- **Strong for English learners.** Speak launched in South Korea in 2019 as an app for speaking English, and English remains a big part of what it does.

If you are a beginner in one of Speak's languages and you want to get over the fear of speaking, it is a very good place to start.

## Where LangX differs from Speak

### Real people instead of an AI

An AI tutor is patient and always on, but it is still an AI. It will not tell you about its city, share a joke that only works in its language, or ask you a question you did not see coming. Real conversation with a native speaker is unpredictable, and that unpredictability is the skill you eventually need. On LangX every person you see in Discover speaks the language you are learning and is learning a language you speak, so both of you get something from every chat.

### Corrections from a human, unlimited

On LangX your partner holds any message to correct it, and corrections are unlimited on every plan, including Free. A human correction often comes with the "why" and with the more natural way a local would say it, not just the grammatically correct one. You correct them in return, which is also a great way to notice patterns in your own language.

### Many more languages

Speak focuses on a handful of major languages. LangX lists 182 languages, so if you are learning something outside Speak's list, a language exchange may be your main option for speaking practice.

### Open source, and a real free plan

The LangX app and API are public on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx) under the BSD-3 license, so anyone can read the code or host their own instance. Speak is closed source. LangX Free is a real plan, not a trial: unlimited text messages, unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations and 20 translations per rolling 24 hours. The [plans page](/pro) has the details.

### No ads, and honest analytics

LangX shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. It does have product analytics (PostHog on its EU cloud, including a masked screen recording on iOS and Android where every word and image is replaced by a grey block on the device). It is on by default and switches off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) lists exactly what is sent.

### What LangX does not do

- **No AI tutor today.** LangX Copilot, private AI feedback while you practice, is planned for Polyglot but has not shipped yet.
- **No lessons or drills.** If you cannot yet form a sentence, LangX will not teach you how.
- **No live calls,** only voice messages.
- **Humans are not instant.** Your partner has a life and a time zone, and a smaller community means fewer people for some language pairs.

## Is LangX a free Speak alternative?

For speaking with real people, yes. LangX Free has no time limit and no ads, and the part that teaches, corrections and replies, is never rationed. What it is not is a free version of Speak's AI tutor or course. If that is what you want, you will need Speak itself or another AI app.

## LangX vs Speak: who should pick which?

**Pick Speak if you:**

- are a beginner who needs structure and a lot of guided speaking
- feel too anxious to talk to a person yet
- want instant pronunciation feedback at any hour
- are learning one of Speak's languages

**Pick LangX if you:**

- already know the basics and want real conversations with native speakers
- want an open source alternative to Speak you can audit or self-host
- want unlimited human corrections without paying
- are learning a language Speak does not cover

**Or use both.** This is what we would suggest to most learners:

1. Rehearse a scenario with Speak's AI until you can say it without thinking.
2. Use the same phrases that day in a LangX chat with a native speaker.
3. Save your partner's corrections and bring them back to your next AI session.

The AI builds confidence; the human tests it. Our guide on [AI conversation partners vs real language exchange](/ai-conversation-partner-vs-language-exchange) goes deeper, and [how to do a language exchange](/how-to-do-a-language-exchange) covers your first chats.

## FAQ

### What is the best open source alternative to Speak?

If what you want is speaking practice with real people, LangX is an open source alternative to Speak: its app and API are BSD-3 licensed on GitHub and can be self-hosted. It is a language exchange, not an AI tutor, so it does not replace Speak's lessons.

### Is there a free Speak alternative?

LangX's Free plan has no time limit and no ads, and includes unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day. You practice with native speakers rather than an AI. Speak itself offers a free trial, after which a subscription is needed.

### LangX vs Speak: which is better for speaking?

Speak is better for guided, high-volume speaking drills with instant AI feedback, especially for beginners. LangX is better for real conversations with native speakers once you know the basics. Neither has live calls with humans; LangX uses voice messages.

### Are there apps like Speak with real people instead of AI?

Yes. Language exchange apps like LangX, Tandem and HelloTalk connect you with native speakers, and tutor marketplaces like italki connect you with paid teachers. The [best language exchange apps](/best-language-exchange-apps) roundup compares the free options.

### Can I use Speak and LangX together?

Yes, and it works well. Practice a scenario with Speak's AI, then use it with a real partner on LangX the same day and let them correct you.

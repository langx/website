If you are looking for an open source alternative to Praktika that lets you practice with real people rather than AI avatars, LangX is a free, open source (BSD-3) language exchange app that matches you with native speakers who can correct any message you send. Praktika is the better choice if you want a patient AI tutor with lessons, instant feedback and no other humans involved, so in the LangX vs Praktika question the two apps really cover different stages of learning.

We make LangX, so read this with that in mind. Everything about Praktika below comes from its official site, its app store listings and independent reviews at the time of writing (September 2026), when Praktika was active on iOS and Android.

## Praktika and LangX are different kinds of app

[Praktika](https://praktika.ai) is an **AI tutor app with animated avatars**. It is based in London, was founded by the team behind the conversational AI company Cleverbots, and started out focused on English before adding more languages in 2024. You pick a tutor, for example Raika, Tama, Skye, Camila or Min-Jun, and practice speaking with a lifelike 3D character that talks back, remembers your context and corrects your pronunciation, grammar and word choice as you go. Around the conversations sit learning paths (travel, career, exam prep such as IELTS and TOEFL) with grammar, vocabulary and listening exercises.

LangX is a **language exchange app**. There are no avatars, lessons or AI tutors. You are matched with people who speak the language you are learning and are learning a language you speak, and you talk to them, with corrections, translation and voice messages built into the chat.

So when people search for a "Praktika alternative", they usually want one of two things:

1. **A different AI tutor.** LangX is not that; Speak is the closest rival.
2. **Real people to talk to** once the AI has warmed them up. That is what LangX is for.

## What Praktika does well

Praktika has made AI tutoring feel unusually personal, and a fair Praktika alternative page should say so.

- **The closest thing to a private tutor on your phone.** Avatars look at you, react and keep the thread of the conversation. For many learners, that makes speaking practice feel much less like talking to a machine.
- **Feedback you can tune.** You can set feedback to soft, balanced or strict, and corrections arrive without stopping the conversation.
- **Built for nervous speakers.** Nobody is judging you. You can repeat a sentence until it sounds right, at any hour.
- **Structured paths.** Goal-based paths for travel, living abroad, career growth and exams like IELTS and TOEFL give beginners a clear next step.
- **Help in your own language.** Tutors can guide you in your native language at the start, which lowers the barrier for true beginners.
- **Upload your own material.** Praktika lets you share photos, audio, video or documents and talk about them or get them corrected, which is handy for real-world tasks.
- **Especially strong for English.** Praktika began as an English-speaking app, and English learners remain a big part of its audience.

For a beginner who is too anxious to talk to a person yet, Praktika is a genuinely good first step.

## Where LangX differs from Praktika

### Real humans, two-way matching

However good an avatar is, it is still generating replies to please you. A native speaker on LangX has their own opinions, slang, humor and time zone, and will ask you things no lesson prepared you for. Every profile in Discover speaks the language you are learning and is learning a language you speak, so the exchange is balanced: you help them, they help you.

### Corrections from people, unlimited on every plan

On LangX your partner taps any message to correct it, and corrections are unlimited on every plan, Free included. A human usually tells you what a local would actually say, not only what is grammatically right. You also learn a lot by correcting their messages in your own language.

### Far more languages

Praktika covers nine languages. LangX lists 182, so if you are learning, say, Turkish, Hindi or Tagalog, a language exchange is often the practical way to get speaking practice.

### Open source and a free plan with no time limit

The LangX app and API are public on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx) under the BSD-3 license, and you can host your own instance. Praktika is closed source. LangX Free is not a trial: unlimited text messages, unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations and 20 translations per rolling 24 hours. Paid plans lift the limits; see [plans](/pro).

### No ads

LangX shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. It does use product analytics (PostHog on its EU cloud, including a masked screen recording on iOS and Android that replaces every word and image with a grey block on the device). It is on by default and you can switch it off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) has the details.

### What LangX does not do

- **No AI tutor today.** LangX Copilot, private AI feedback while you practice, is planned for Polyglot but not shipped.
- **No lessons, paths or exam prep.**
- **No live calls,** only voice messages.
- **People are not always online,** and a smaller community means fewer partners for some language pairs.

## Is LangX a free Praktika alternative?

If "free" means ongoing speaking practice without a subscription, yes: LangX Free has no time limit, no ads and unlimited corrections. It is not a free version of Praktika's avatar tutor, though. You are trading AI availability and structure for real people.

## LangX vs Praktika: who should pick which?

**Pick Praktika if you:**

- are a beginner and want a guided path with lots of speaking
- feel too shy to talk to a stranger yet
- want instant, adjustable feedback on every sentence
- are preparing for an exam like IELTS or TOEFL

**Pick LangX if you:**

- can already hold a basic conversation and want real native speakers
- want an open source alternative to Praktika you can audit or self-host
- want unlimited human corrections on a free plan
- learn a language Praktika does not offer

**Or combine them.** Rehearse a situation with a Praktika avatar, then have the same conversation for real with a LangX partner the same day, and bring their corrections back to your next AI session. ## FAQ

### What is the best open source alternative to Praktika?

For speaking practice with real people, LangX is an open source alternative to Praktika. Its app and API are BSD-3 licensed on GitHub and can be self-hosted. It is a language exchange rather than an AI tutor, so it does not replace Praktika's avatars or lessons.

### Is there a free Praktika alternative?

LangX has a free plan with no time limit and no ads: unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day, with native speakers instead of an AI. Praktika itself is free to download, but full access requires a subscription.

### LangX vs Praktika: which is better for speaking?

Praktika is better for guided practice and confidence-building with instant AI feedback. LangX is better for real conversations with native speakers once you have the basics. Many learners use Praktika first and add LangX as they progress.

Yes. Language exchange apps such as LangX, Tandem and HelloTalk connect you with native speakers for free, and tutor sites like italki connect you with paid teachers. See the [best language exchange apps](/best-language-exchange-apps).

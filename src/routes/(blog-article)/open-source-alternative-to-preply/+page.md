If you want a free, open source alternative to Preply for conversation practice, LangX is a language exchange app with public code (BSD-3) that matches you with native speakers who are learning your language, with corrections on any message, unlimited on every plan. It does not replace a Preply tutor: Preply is a marketplace for paid one-to-one lessons, and LangX is for the free peer practice you do between those lessons.

## Preply and LangX are different kinds of service

**Preply** is a tutor marketplace. You browse tutors, filter by language, price, reviews, specialty and availability, book a trial lesson, and if you like the tutor you subscribe to a set number of lessons with them. Its app lists lessons in more than 90 languages, and the platform also covers subjects beyond languages. It also sells corporate language training for companies.

**LangX** is a free language exchange app. There are no tutors and no lessons with a teacher; what you can study alone is Echo, free spaced-repetition packs of phrases in six languages. You are matched with someone who speaks the language you are learning and is learning a language you speak, and you help each other in a one-to-one chat with corrections, translation and voice messages built in.

So a "Preply alternative" can mean two things:

1. **Another tutoring marketplace** (for example italki or Cambly), if you want a professional teacher. LangX is not that.
2. **A free way to keep practicing between lessons**, or before you commit to paying for them. That is what LangX is for.

## What Preply does well

Preply is one of the largest tutoring platforms, and for many goals it is the right tool:

- **A huge choice of tutors.** Many languages and a wide range of tutors, including for less common languages. You can filter by price, reviews, specialty and availability, so you can find someone who fits both your goal and your budget. Tutors set their own rates.
- **A real teacher's attention.** A tutor can diagnose your mistakes, plan a path, explain grammar and push you in ways a peer usually cannot. For exams, job interviews, relocation or business language, that matters.
- **Built-in accountability.** At the time of writing, Preply sells lessons as a subscription with one tutor: a fixed number of lessons per week, billed every 28 days. Preply says subscriptions can be paused, changed or canceled. That structure keeps a lot of people consistent.
- **A low-risk start.** You book a trial lesson first, 25 or 50 minutes. If the tutor is not right for you, Preply offers a free replacement trial with another tutor, within limits set out in its help center.
- **Live speaking.** Lessons are live video, so you practice speaking and listening in real time, which is exactly what text chat cannot fully replace.

If you are working toward a deadline and can budget for it, a good Preply tutor is one of the fastest ways to improve.

## Where LangX differs from Preply

### Free peer practice between lessons

A subscription usually means one or two lessons a week. The rest of the week is where vocabulary fades and confidence slips. LangX is built for that gap: short, daily conversations with someone who speaks your target language natively and wants help with yours. Our post on [why consistent practice is key](/why-consistent-practice-is-key-to-language-learning-success) explains why small daily sessions add up, and [how to remember a language even after years](/stop-the-struggle-how-to-remember-your-foreign-languages-even-after-years) covers keeping it once you have it.

### An open source alternative to Preply

The LangX app and API are public on [GitHub](https://github.com/langx/langx) under the BSD-3 license. You can read the code, see what the app sends, contribute, or host your own instance. Preply is closed source. If transparency matters to you, that is a reason to look for an open source alternative to Preply, at least for the practice side of learning.

### Nobody pays anybody

On Preply you pay for a tutor's time. On LangX the person correcting you is someone you help in return, so it costs nothing but your time and attention. That also means you do half the teaching, which many learners enjoy and some do not. [How to do a language exchange](/how-to-do-a-language-exchange) shows how to split the time fairly.

### Corrections you can review

On LangX you hold any message to correct it, and corrections are unlimited on every plan, Free included, as are replies to anyone who writes to you. Because the conversation is written down, every correction stays in the chat. Translation and read-aloud are there when you need them.

### Not live, and not a teacher

LangX has no live calls; conversations are text, voice, photo and video messages. And a partner is not a trained teacher: they know what sounds natural but may not be able to explain why. If you need structured lessons, grammar explained or exam preparation, keep your tutor.

### No ads

LangX shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. It does have product analytics (PostHog on its EU cloud, including a masked screen recording on iOS and Android where every word and image is replaced by a grey block on the device). It is on by default and switches off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) lists exactly what is sent.

## Is LangX a free Preply alternative?

For practice, yes; for lessons, no. LangX Free includes unlimited text messages, unlimited replies and corrections, 5 new conversations a day and 20 translations a day, with no ads. Fluent and Polyglot add unlimited new conversations, more translations, more languages on your profile and extra filters; see the [plans page](/plans). Prices are set per region and shown in the app. No plan includes a tutor.

## LangX vs Preply: who should pick which?

**Pick Preply if you:**

- want a professional tutor who plans lessons around your goals
- are preparing for an exam, a job or a move abroad
- want live video speaking practice every week
- like the accountability of a fixed weekly slot

**Pick LangX if you:**

- want a free Preply alternative for daily practice between lessons
- want an open source app you can audit or self-host
- are happy to help someone with your language in return
- want corrections, translation and read-aloud inside a chat

**Or combine them.** A tutor once or twice a week for structure and a LangX partner every day for volume is a strong setup. ## FAQ

### Is there an open source alternative to Preply?

We know of no open source tutoring marketplace like Preply. LangX is an open source alternative to Preply for the practice side: a free language exchange app, public under the BSD-3 license, where you chat with native speakers who are learning your language.

### What is the best free Preply alternative?

For free conversation practice, a language exchange app. LangX's Free plan has no ads and includes unlimited replies and corrections and 5 new conversations a day. It does not include lessons or a tutor.

### LangX vs Preply: can LangX replace a tutor?

No. Preply gives you a tutor who plans and runs lessons; LangX gives you a peer to practice with for free. Many learners get the best results using both: lessons for guidance, an exchange for daily practice.

### Does LangX have live video lessons like Preply?

No. LangX has voice, photo and video messages and read-aloud, but no live calls and no teachers.

italki and Cambly are the closest apps like Preply. For free practice with native speakers, language exchange apps such as LangX, Tandem and HelloTalk. The [compare page](/compare) lists every LangX comparison.
